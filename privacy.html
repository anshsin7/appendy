Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Appendy ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your information when you use our website and service (the "Service"). We are committed to handling your data responsibly and in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP).

The short version: We only collect your email address (for your account) and basic payment confirmation (via Stripe). Your actual documents are processed in your browser and are only stored if you are signed in — and even then, only in your private account space. We never sell your data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for the Service is the operator of Appendy. For any privacy question or request, contact us at justanshhere@gmail.com. (Replace this with your real contact email.)

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address (and, for Google sign-in, your name and Google account identifier). This is used to create and manage your account.
  • Document data: the PDF and image files you add. When you are signed in, these may be stored in your private cloud storage so you can access them again. Files are never shared with other users.
  • Payment data: if you purchase Lifetime access, payment is processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation that you paid and your plan status — we never see or store your full card details.
  • Technical data: standard information your browser sends (such as device/browser type) and anonymous usage analytics to help us improve the Service.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the Service and keep you signed in across sessions and devices.
  • To store and sync your document sets and account settings.
  • To process your purchase and activate Lifetime access.
  • To communicate important account or service information.
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), to comply with legal obligations (such as tax/accounting for payments), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and improving the Service.

5. Where your data is stored

Account and document data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and, where enabled, Cloud Storage). Payments are handled by Stripe. These providers may process data on servers located inside and outside Switzerland/the EU, under appropriate safeguards.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and document data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account or ask us to delete your data, we remove it, except where we must keep certain records (for example, payment records required for accounting and tax).

7. Your rights

Under GDPR and Swiss law you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at justanshhere@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use essential browser storage (such as local storage) to keep you signed in and to cache your document sets for performance. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how the Service is used. We do not use advertising-tracking cookies.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you.
