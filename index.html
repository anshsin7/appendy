Questions

Frequently asked

Is Appendy free to use?

Yes. The Free plan lets you create up to 2 document sets with files up to 10 MB each, and you can merge and download PDFs. Lifetime access (one payment of CHF 9.90) removes those limits forever.

How do I merge PDFs with Appendy?

Sign in, create a document set, drag in your PDFs and images, reorder them however you like, then click “Combine & download” to get a single merged PDF.

Are my files safe and private?

Your files are processed in your browser and are only stored in your own private account space. They are never shared with other users or sold. See our Privacy Policy.

What file types are supported?

PDF, PNG and JPG. Images are automatically placed on their own page when merged.

Is the Lifetime plan really a one-time payment?

Yes — CHF 9.90 once, no subscription, no recurring charges. It unlocks unlimited document sets and removes the file-size limit.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on Lifetime purchases. See our Refund Policy.
