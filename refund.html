Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: You can try Appendy free before you buy. If you purchase Lifetime access and it doesn't work for you, contact us within 14 days and we'll refund you.

1. Try before you buy

The Free plan lets you test Appendy's core features — including combining and downloading PDFs — before paying. We encourage you to make sure the Service meets your needs before purchasing Lifetime access.

2. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you purchase Lifetime access and are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase. Simply email us from the address on your account and we'll process it, usually within a few business days.

3. How to request a refund

Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with the email address used for your account and, if possible, your Stripe receipt or payment date. (Replace with your real contact email.)

4. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via Stripe. Depending on your bank, it may take a few business days for the amount to appear.

5. After a refund

Once refunded, your account returns to the Free plan and Lifetime features are deactivated.

6. Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection law, which are not affected by this policy.
